FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Can I start with just one module?

Absolutely. Start with HRM or CRM and switch on other modules whenever you're ready. Your data is already connected, so new modules work instantly.

How long does it take to migrate from our current tools?

Most teams go live in 1–3 weeks. We offer free data import from spreadsheets, Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Tally, QuickBooks, SAP Business One and more.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit, hosted in regional data centers, and we're SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliant.

Do you support local tax and payroll compliance?

Nexora supports GST, VAT and statutory payroll rules for 40+ countries, with localized reports and filings.

Can I customize fields and workflows?

Yes — add custom fields, forms, approval chains and automations with our no-code builder, or extend via REST API and webhooks.
